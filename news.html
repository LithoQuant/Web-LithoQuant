LithoQuant in Its First Professional Course

LithoQuant will be featured in Petrophysics in Practice, a two-day professional course at the 55th PIT IAGI Post-Convention. Participants will explore well logging, petrophysical interpretation, and reservoir evaluation with hands-on use of LithoQuant.

The course takes place on 6–7 November 2026 at Hotel Ambarukmo, Yogyakarta, with instructors Yana Hendrayana and Miftahul Firdaus.

July 2026

LithoQuant v1.1 - Geomechanics & Rock Physics Suite

We are proud to announce LithoQuant v1.1, introducing full 1D geomechanics and advanced rock physics modules. Engineers can now model pore pressure using multiple methods (Eaton, Equivalent Depth, Tau), calculate horizontal and effective stresses, map faulting regimes, establish safe mud weight windows, and perform comprehensive rock physics diagnostics directly from log data.
